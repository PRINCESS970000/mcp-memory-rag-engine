# BrightPeak Grade Appeal Policy

## Section 1 — Appeal Window
A student may appeal a grade within 14 calendar days of their enrollment
status being set to COMPLETED with that grade recorded. Appeals filed
after this window are rejected automatically, unless Section 4 applies.

## Section 2 — Appeal Process

### Section 2.1 — Submission
An appeal must reference the enrollment_id, the current recorded grade,
and a written justification.

### Section 2.2 — Outcome
An approved appeal results in the grade being updated on the same
enrollment_id. The previous grade value is never deleted; it is retained
in an audit log alongside the corrected value and both dates, see the
Certificate Reissue Policy, Section 4, for how this interacts with a
certificate that was already issued.

## Section 3 — Grounds for Appeal
Valid grounds include a data-entry mistake, a miscalculated grade, or
work that was submitted but not recorded. A disagreement with an
instructor's subjective judgment of quality is not valid grounds under
this section.

## Section 4 — Role-Based Extended Window
A student whose account role is TA or ADMIN, and who is also enrolled as
a student in the course being appealed, has an extended appeal window of
30 calendar days instead of 14, since staff duties may delay their review
of a posted grade.

## Section 5 — Who May Resolve an Appeal
Only the instructor assigned to the course, or an account with role
ADMIN, may approve or reject a grade appeal. See the Grading Permissions Policy, Section 2, for the full rule on who may update a grade.
